Empowering Developers: Onboarding & Mentorship

Neue Mitarbeiter · Talent Development · Munich, Germany

A high-performing software team is not just about writing clean algorithms—it is fundamentally about how quickly and comfortably new teammates can contribute with confidence. As listed in my top competencies on LinkedIn (“Neue Mitarbeiter” & “Teamführung”), developing people is one of the most fulfilling parts of engineering leadership.

When hiring and onboarding new software engineers at CHECK24, my goal has always been to transform what can often be an overwhelming transition into a structured, welcoming, and high-trust experience.

The Onboarding Playbook

  • Day-One Production Deployment: Designed automated local Docker setup scripts and curated starter tickets so every new engineer merges their first well-tested code change to production within their very first week.
  • Buddy System & Safe Failure: Assigned dedicated onboarding buddies for daily check-ins, actively encouraging questions and fostering a blameless mindset where mistakes are treated as learning opportunities.
  • Architecture & Domain Roadmaps: Replaced monolithic documentation with digestible, step-by-step interactive walkthroughs of system boundaries, database schemas, and business workflows.
  • Career Growth Pathways: Conducted bi-weekly 1-on-1s focusing not just on sprint tasks, but on personal aspirations, technical challenges, and leadership opportunities.
